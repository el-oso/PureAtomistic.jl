A vacancy in hot Cu ​

One vacancy in a 4 × 4 × 4 block of fcc cells (255 atoms) of Cu with MACE-MPA-0 at 1300 K, run at constant volume with the Bussi thermostat, and followed by a VacancyTracker.

This is an illustration of the tracker on a molecular-dynamics run, not a measurement of a diffusion coefficient or a migration barrier. One cell, one temperature and one trajectory of 22 ps give a handful of hops at most; their count carries a large statistical error (a Poisson count of n has a relative error of 1/√n), the cell is held at the lattice constant of the zero-temperature crystal rather than at its thermal expansion at 1300 K, and the tracker's window and dwell are chosen by hand. The campaign over a range of temperatures that would give an Arrhenius fit is not part of this page.

This page does not run the dynamics. It reads bench/results/md_vacancy_hop.json, which holds the tracker's record together with the commit, the date and the settings, and plots it.

julia
using CairoMakie, JSON, Printf, PureAtomistic
const RESULTS = joinpath(pkgdir(PureAtomistic), "bench", "results")
d = JSON.parsefile(joinpath(RESULTS, "md_vacancy_hop.json"))
s = d["settings"]
@printf(
    "commit %s, %s, Julia %s, BLAS %s on %d thread, %d atoms, %g K, dt = %g fs, seed %d\n",
    s["commit"], s["date"], s["julia"], s["blas"], s["blas_threads"], s["natoms"], s["temperature_K"], s["dt_fs"], s["seed"]
)
@printf(
    "%s: %d steps = %.2f ps in %.0f s of wall time (budget %.0f s); mean kinetic temperature %.0f K\n",
    d["status"], d["steps"], d["duration_ps"], s["wall_seconds"], s["wall_budget_seconds"], d["mean_temperature_K"]
)
@printf(
    "tracker: frame every %g fs, positions averaged over %d frames, a new site must hold for %d frames\n",
    s["frame_interval_fs"], s["tracker_window_frames"], s["tracker_dwell_frames"]
)
@printf("confirmed hops: %d, at t = %s fs\n", d["hops"], join(map(t -> @sprintf("%.0f", t), d["hop_times_fs"]), ", "))
isempty(d["message"]) || println("run stopped by: ", d["message"])
true

The track ​

The unwrapped position of the vacancy and its squared displacement from the starting site, with a dashed line at each confirmed hop. A hop of the vacancy to a nearest-neighbor site is a displacement of a/√2 ≈ 2.57 Å, so its squared displacement is a²/2 ≈ 6.6 Å². The squared displacement of one trajectory is not a mean-square displacement and is not fitted.

julia
t = Float64.(d["t_fs"]) ./ 1000
R = reduce(hcat, [Float64.(r) for r in d["vacancy_R"]])
hops = Float64.(d["hop_times_fs"]) ./ 1000
fig = Figure(; size = (900, 520), fontsize = 13)
ax1 = Axis(fig[1, 1]; ylabel = "vacancy position (Å)")
for (c, name) in enumerate(("x", "y", "z"))
    lines!(ax1, t, R[c, :]; label = name)
end
isempty(hops) || vlines!(ax1, hops; color = (:gray, 0.7), linestyle = :dash)
axislegend(ax1; position = :lt)
ax2 = Axis(fig[2, 1]; xlabel = "time (ps)", ylabel = "squared displacement (Å²)")
lines!(ax2, t, [sum(abs2, R[:, k] - R[:, 1]) for k in axes(R, 2)])
isempty(hops) || vlines!(ax2, hops; color = (:gray, 0.7), linestyle = :dash)
linkxaxes!(ax1, ax2)
save("vacancy_track.png", fig)

Animation ​

The atoms of the cell (small, colored by height) and the vacancy site (large red sphere), with the unwrapped vacancy position up to the time shown. The animation is rendered separately by docs/render_animations.jl from the trajectory of the run, which is not stored in the repository, and saved to docs/src/assets/.
