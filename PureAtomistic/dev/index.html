PureAtomistic.jl ​

Molecular dynamics on top of PureMLIP potentials, in pure Julia.

The package is organized in layers: Systems holds the atomic configuration, Potentials evaluates energies and forces, Integrators, Thermostats and Barostats advance it, Analysis reads the results, and IO reads and writes configurations.

The example gallery shows the validation runs of the molecular-dynamics driver: energy conservation, canonical sampling with the Bussi thermostat, and a vacancy at 1300 K.
